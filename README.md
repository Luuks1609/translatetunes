# TranslateTunes

Welcome to TranslateTunes! This is a fun and interactive platform where you can enhance your language skills by translating the lyrics of your favorite songs. Earn stars as you play through different levels and challenges!

## Getting Started

To begin your journey with TranslateTunes, follow the steps below:

### Step 1: Login

First, you need to login to access your profile and start using the app.

- Visit the following URL: [https://translatetunes.web.app](https://translatetunes.web.app)
- If you are not already logged in, you will be prompted to do so.

### Step 2: Authenticate Your Spotify Account

After logging in, you need to authenticate your Spotify account to access your favorite songs.

- Once logged in, you'll see an option to connect your Spotify account.
- Follow the prompts to authenticate and allow TranslateTunes to access your Spotify playlists.

### Step 3: Browse Your Favorite Songs

With your Spotify account connected, you can now browse through your favorite songs.

- Choose a song you love and start translating the lyrics.
- Each song provides a unique learning experience with different levels.

### Step 4: Play Levels and Earn Stars

As you translate, you'll play through different levels designed to test your understanding.

- Complete each level successfully to earn stars.
- Display golden badges on your profile by completing all 3 levels.

## Need Help?

If you encounter any issues or have questions, feel free to open an issue in this repository or contact us directly.

Enjoy your learning journey with TranslateTunes!
